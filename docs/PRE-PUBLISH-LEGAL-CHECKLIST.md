# Germany launch check — review before publishing

These templates are **not a legal certificate** and are intentionally noindex until verified.

- Confirm the provider's complete serviceable physical address, entity status, correct email and telephone. No PO box. § 5 DDG replaces older § 5 TMG.
- Verify whether any trade register, professional title, supervision, register number, USt-IdNr./Wirtschafts-ID, or editorial responsibility under § 18(2) MStV applies.
- Confirm § 19 UStG Kleinunternehmer status with tax advisor. Invoices must not list VAT separately if the rule applies; use "Kein Steuerausweis aufgrund der Anwendung der Kleinunternehmerregelung (§ 19 UStG)" when verified. Check 2025+ invoicing requirements.
- Verify the actual infrastructure: **current Vercel**, not Wix. Check Vercel DPA, configured retention, regions, optional GA ID, cookies, external fonts, email provider and subprocessors. If migrated to Wix, re-document all processors and apps; recheck consent banner and log retention.
- Ensure Impressum and Datenschutz are reachable from every site page, preferably footer.
- Validate privacy notice against actual collection and back-end endpoints; read test event logs/cookies in browser. Optional analytics requires prior consent and revocation (GDPR / § 25 TDDDG).
- Check contractual arrangements: pricing transparency and applicable consumer cancellation/Widerruf obligations if remote contracts are actually concluded, applicable VSBG notices, marketing claims/credentials permissions, accessibility duties if applicable.
- Obtain owner/qualified German legal review **before** public launch. Remove the "draft" banners, placeholders and noindex only after sign-off.
- Replace existing public certificate preview asset with supplied revised, truly redacted PDF and regenerate thumbnail only after securely erasing identifying details, including certificate number. Current branch still contains the earlier PNG; do not assume replacement has happened.
